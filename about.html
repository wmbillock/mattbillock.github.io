About

Hi, I'm Willow

I'm a staff-level software engineer and a brass player, and most of what I build sits somewhere between those two.

Engineering

I'm a polyglot who works in Python, Go and JavaScript every day: Django, Flask, FastAPI and SQLAlchemy on the back end, React on the front. I like staff-level problems: architecture, high-reliability systems, and the kind of technical leadership that cuts across teams.

Lately that means agent systems. I build multi-agent orchestration and production LLM tooling, usually organized the way an ensemble is: clear roles, a chain of command, and a shared score. I also have a long history of running my own infrastructure, which keeps me honest about what it takes to keep software up.

Music

I play euphonium, tenor horn, baritone, cornet, trumpet, flugelhorn, mellophone and tuba, plus valve trombone in a ska band. I came up through Drum Corps International and spent many years in Drum Corps Associates, and I've played championship-section brass band literature at NABBA, including the solo baritone and solo tenor horn chairs.

Off the stage, I've done nonprofit leadership with the Chicago Brass Band and the North American Brass Band Association, and I founded the Windy City Brass Society for youth brass and percussion players. I'm especially interested in building technology for the classical music world.
